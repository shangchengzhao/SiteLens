**SiteLens** is a map-based workspace for efficiently verifying location and facility data. Users upload a CSV containing addresses or coordinates, and SiteLens automatically locates each record on an interactive Google Map while highlighting relevant facilities in the surrounding area, including industrial and logistics sites, retail and hospitality businesses, medical and educational institutions, offices, and other commercial or institutional properties. Satellite imagery, Street View, nearby business information, and a configurable verification lens help reviewers determine what occupies a target site and what facilities are nearby. Reviewers can move sequentially through records using Previous and Next controls, classify or flag each location, add business names and notes, and record verification outcomes. All review results are appended to the original dataset and can be downloaded as an enriched CSV when verification is complete.
